Botanical Heritage

The True Cinnamon of Sri Lanka

Ceylon cinnamon, botanically known as Cinnamomum verum, is native to Sri Lanka and is valued for its delicate aroma, refined flavour and distinctive quills made from many thin layers of inner bark.

Ceylon Harvest specialises in authentic true Ceylon cinnamon — Alba, C4 and C5 grades — selected from Sri Lanka and brought to customers across the United Kingdom.

Quality Distinction

True Cinnamon, Not Cassia

Understanding the fundamental differences ensures you experience the genuine aroma and delicate profile of authentic Sri Lankan cinnamon.

Exclusive Offering

True Ceylon Cinnamon

  • Botanically known as Cinnamomum verum.
  • Origin: Sri Lanka (the true home of cinnamon).
  • Fine, light, multi-layered quills resembling delicate parchment.
  • Delicate aroma and refined, warm flavour.
  • The only cinnamon sold by Ceylon Harvest.

Cassia Cinnamon

  • Not sold by Ceylon Harvest.
  • Commonly darker, thicker, harder and coarser.
  • Often formed from a single thick, heavy bark curl.
  • Visually and aromatically distinct from true Ceylon cinnamon.
